AI Readiness & Workforce Transition Index

Business Adaptation, Workforce Transformation and Operational AI Integration Across Emerging Enterprises

Author: Priyanshu Gupta · Lovely Professional University · Bachelor of Business Administration — Final Year

This project is an independent analytical and educational research initiative. It is not an official university publication, peer-reviewed academic work, government report, empirical survey, or validated benchmarking instrument.

Executive Summary

The AI Readiness and Workforce Transition Index examines how emerging enterprises are managing the concurrent pressures of operational AI integration and workforce adaptation. The framework organises readiness across Strategic Intent, Digital Operations, Data Readiness, and Workforce Adaptability, each weighted equally to produce a Composite Index of 60 out of 100.

Methodology and Limitations

This is an independent analytical research project, not an empirical study with primary data collection. The analysis synthesises published institutional research to construct a conceptual framework that has not been validated through field surveys, expert panels, or psychometric testing.
